About Us

A Karachi tradition, served with heart.

At Mulla Nehari House, we bring the authentic taste of Karachi's traditional food culture to your plate. Our Nehari is prepared using time-honored recipes, slow-cooked to perfection with rich spices and tender meat.

Located in the heart of PIB Colony, Ghousia Colony, Karachi, we take pride in serving freshly prepared Nehari that reflects the true essence of Pakistani cuisine. Every dish is crafted with care, using quality ingredients and traditional cooking methods.

Whether you're dining with family or ordering for a gathering, our commitment to authentic flavors and warm hospitality ensures a memorable experience.
